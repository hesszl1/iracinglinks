iRacing Links

SETUP RESOURCES

Setups and utilities for setups. Includes setup shops.

Website Discipline(s) Description
Team Watson Racing Setups All Setup shop for a wide variety of cars spanning across all disciplines.
VRS (Virtual Racing School) All Paid setups for most open series. Seen as a step above baseline setups. Also has data packs and tutorials.
Apex Racing Academy Road/Rallycross Similar to VRS with a focus on road racing and Rallycross.
GO Setups/Craig's Setup Shop Road Has setups for all the modern popular racing sims. Craig from Craig's Setup Shop is part of the iRacing side of things.
Maconi Setup Shop Oval/Dirt Oval/Sports Car One of the most widespread oval setup shops. Sponsored C-Fixed for a while.
Garage 61 All Free service that allows you to compare setups from the fastest lap times. Paid setups are locked behind a paywall.
Majors Garage All Free baseline+ setups. Requires an account to download them. Better setups are paid.
iRacing Setup Viewer N/A A basic tool to view and compare exported iRacing setups.
